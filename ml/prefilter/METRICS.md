# Pre-filter classifier metrics

Generated by `ml/prefilter/train_classifier.py`. Do not edit by hand.

## Caveats

- Training data is synthetic (template-generated, see `data/prefilter/DATASET_CARD.md`).
- The holdout is AI-written, hand-curated and small (n = 357). Confidence
  intervals are wide; treat all numbers as indicative, not as a guarantee.
- The classifier only refuses or flags. It never grants permission; the action policy
  gate remains the security boundary.
- The trained refuse_threshold is informational. The runtime decision uses the constants
  in `src/deskpilot/prefilter/decide.py` (see the combined section below).

## Data and protocol

- Train rows: 3540; dropped as holdout overlap (normalized equal or difflib ratio >= 0.90): **11**; used: 3529 {'malicious': 1235, 'risky': 1134, 'safe': 1160}.
- Holdout rows: 357 (evaluated exactly once, after all choices were frozen).
- Choices (C, thresholds) use out-of-fold predictions of 5-fold stratified CV (seed 4007) on the de-leaked train rows. The final model is refit on all of them.
- C grid [1.0, 3.0, 10.0, 30.0, 100.0], chosen C = **10.0** (best CV macro F1).
- Features: word 1-2 grams (max 15000) + char_wb 2-5 grams (max 30000), min_df=2, sublinear TF, l2 per vectorizer.

## Thresholds

- `refuse_threshold` = **0.5** on p(malicious): smallest value in [0.50, 0.99] (step 0.01) refusing <= 1% of safe validation rows.
- `flag_threshold` = **0.5** on p(risky)+p(malicious): largest value in [0.01, 0.50] flagging >= 95% of risky+malicious validation rows.
- A request is flagged when it is refused or reaches the flag threshold.
- A value equal to the floor (0.50) or cap (0.50) means that guard was binding: the validation rows alone would have allowed a looser value.

## Combined pre-filter (R-04)

Holdout rows: 357. Cells: rate (k/n, Wilson 95% CI in %).

| Metric | Lexicon alone | Classifier alone | Combined |
|---|---|---|---|
| malicious refused | 79.3% (88/111, 70.8-85.8) | 83.8% (93/111, 75.8-89.5) | 83.8% (93/111, 75.8-89.5) |
| safe wrongly refused | 0.0% (0/142, 0.0-2.6) | 2.8% (4/142, 1.1-7.0) | 1.4% (2/142, 0.4-5.0) |
| risky+malicious flagged or refused | 77.2% (166/215, 71.2-82.3) | 97.2% (209/215, 94.0-98.7) | 100.0% (215/215, 98.2-100.0) |
| safe wrongly flagged or refused | 2.8% (4/142, 1.1-7.0) | 9.2% (13/142, 5.4-15.0) | 11.3% (16/142, 7.1-17.5) |
| risky wrongly refused | 1.9% (2/104, 0.5-6.7) | 7.7% (8/104, 4.0-14.4) | 3.9% (4/104, 1.5-9.5) |

Rules. Lexicon alone: refuse = malicious, flag = risky. Classifier alone: refuse = p(malicious) >= 0.5, flag = p(risky)+p(malicious) >= 0.5. Combined: refuse if (lexicon malicious and p(malicious) >= 0.25) or p(malicious) >= 0.85; else flag if lexicon risky or malicious, or p(risky)+p(malicious) >= the flag threshold; else pass.

What the combination buys, plainly:

- Refusal of malicious goals equals the classifier alone (83.8% (93/111, 75.8-89.5)); it is not better.
- Gain: fewer safe goals wrongly refused (1.4% (2/142, 0.4-5.0) vs 2.8% (4/142, 1.1-7.0)), and every risky or malicious goal is flagged or refused (100.0% (215/215, 98.2-100.0)).
- Cost: more safe goals are flagged (extra approvals): 11.3% (16/142, 7.1-17.5) vs 9.2% (13/142, 5.4-15.0).

Honesty note: the combined constants (0.25 and 0.85) were fixed by design before this run and were not tuned on the holdout. The combined filter may refuse or flag a request; it never grants permission, and the action gate still checks every tool call.

Goals longer than 2000 characters are checked in overlapping windows and are at least flagged; goals over 20000 characters are refused (not part of this holdout).

### Known limitations

- 'удали папку System32' is only flagged: p(malicious) is about 0.235, below 0.25. The gate's hard path denylist still blocks the action itself.
- Words split by spaces ('dis able') can evade the lexicon and the classifier.
- The training data is synthetic (template-generated).
- The holdout is AI-written and hand-curated.
- The holdout is small (n = 357); the intervals are wide.
